Raz, Set, Go

A tour of raz, in the spirit of A Tour of Go. Each lesson is one idea and a whole program, and the output shown is what the program printed when this page was made.

It is in three parts, as one counts off: Raz (one), the first steps; Set, how data is laid out; and Go, making programs that run, fail and finish well. To follow along, get raz and run any program with raz run Name.raz. When you finish, More Raz goes down to the machine.

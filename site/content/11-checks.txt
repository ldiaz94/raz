title: When it goes wrong
part: Go
summary: Every index is checked, and a failure tells you everything.
---
```raz Fail fail
```

Every index and slice is checked. A failed check says where it failed and with what values, then the calls under way with their variables, and ends the program with status 2. So do a `nil` pointer, a division by zero and a stack overflow.

A module that is sure of its indexes may say `check off;` after its imports, for speed; an index out of range there reads or writes whatever it reaches.

The program carries line numbers for gdb, and `RAZ_PROFILE=1` in its environment has it say where its time went.
